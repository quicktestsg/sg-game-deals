Free Games to Claim Right Now — August 2026

Free games are everywhere — if you know where to look. Every platform hands out titles for nothing: Epic gives away a game (or three) every week, PS Plus drops a fresh batch monthly, Xbox has perpetual free-to-play hits plus Game Pass trials, and even the eShop has free gems.

Let's get one thing straight: free games are not a scam, and they're not low-quality throwaways. Platforms give away genuine AAA and indie titles as a customer acquisition play — Epic alone has given away over $3,000 worth of games to date. The catch? They're time-limited. Miss the window and you pay full price.

Epic Games Store — Free Every Thursday

The Epic Games Store is the undisputed king of free game giveaways. Every Thursday at 4 PM GMT, Epic swaps in a new free game (or a mystery triple). You just need a free Epic account — no credit card, no subscription. Once claimed, the game is yours forever.

How to never miss an Epic freebie

  • Claim from the website: Log into epicgames.com, click the free game banner, hit 'Get.' Added instantly.
  • Use a tracker: Sites like EpicGamesDB and r/GameDeals post new freebies the moment they go live.
  • Holiday mega-giveaways: Every December, Epic runs a 12-15 day streak of daily mystery games — often big-budget titles.
💡 Pro Tip

Even if you don't play PC games right now, claim every Epic freebie anyway. Games you collect today are still yours years from now. Zero downside — a free account, a single click, a growing library.

PlayStation Plus — Monthly Free Games

PlayStation Plus Essential members get three free games every month — usually a mix of PS5 and PS4 titles. August 2026's lineup is live. Once added to your library, they're yours as long as your PS Plus stays active. New batch drops the first Tuesday of each month.

  • Essential tier ($9.99/mo): Three monthly games — cheapest way to get free PS5 games.
  • Extra tier ($14.99/mo): Adds 400+ downloadable games catalog. Extraordinary value.
  • Claim even if you won't play now: 'Purchase' free games from the PS App even without a console. They'll be waiting.

Xbox — Free-to-Play Hits & Game Pass

Xbox has the strongest free-to-play ecosystem of any console. No Xbox Live Gold required — every free-to-play game is genuinely free, online multiplayer included.

Best free-to-play games on Xbox right now

  • Fortnite: Battle royale king. Zero build mode is the most accessible entry point.
  • Call of Duty: Warzone: Full battle royale CoD at no cost. Enormous file size.
  • Apex Legends: Fast-paced hero shooter BR. Still receiving regular updates.
  • Rocket League: Soccer with rocket cars. Absurdly fun, fully cross-platform.
  • Overwatch 2: Hero shooter, free for everyone. Great character roster.

For Game Pass, new subscribers can frequently get the first month for $1 — access to 100+ games including day-one first-party releases. Even at full price ($19.99/mo for Ultimate), it's the best deal in gaming if you play regularly.

Nintendo eShop — Free Hidden Gems

The eShop is quieter on the free front, but there are genuine free-to-play titles worth downloading on Switch and Switch 2. These aren't demos — they're full games with optional purchases.

  • Pokémon UNITE: MOBA-style Pokémon battles. Surprisingly deep.
  • Fortnite: On Switch too. Runs surprisingly well on the hardware.
  • Fall Guys: Chaotic 60-player party game. Perfect for quick sessions.
  • Game trials: Nintendo periodically offers full-game trials for a week. Recent ones included first-party titles.

The Strategy: Automate Your Claims

The biggest mistake gamers make is forgetting to claim free games. They check back months later and realize they missed dozens of titles. Build a simple routine:

  • Every Thursday: Claim the Epic free game. 30 seconds from your phone.
  • First Tuesday of the month: Add the three PS Plus games to your library.
  • Check this site: We surface time-sensitive free game alerts in the deals feed above.

Over a year, that routine nets you 50+ free games with a retail value easily exceeding $1,500. All it costs is a few minutes per week. Bookmark this page, check the deals feed daily, and never pay for a game you could have claimed for free.
